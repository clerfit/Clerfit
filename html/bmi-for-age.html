What is BMI for Age?

BMI for Age is a measurement-based screening or posture indicator. The result depends on the inputs and method used by this calculator.

What does the result mean?

Interpret the BMI for Age result in the context of the input values, displayed units and assumptions. Simple measurements cannot diagnose medical conditions. Reference standards and measurement technique matter; consult a qualified professional when concerned.

Calculation methodBMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². Age- and sex-specific z-scores use CDC LMS and extended sigma parameters.

This calculator uses CDC extended BMI-for-age reference values for ages 2–19, with age entered in completed years and months. The result is a screening estimate, not a diagnosis; review growth patterns with a qualified clinician. View the CDC reference method.
