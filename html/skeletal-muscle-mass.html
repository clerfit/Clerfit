Calculate from measured body-composition values

Enter a skeletal-muscle-mass result from a validated assessment (such as a compatible body-composition device or DXA report). There is no universal reliable estimate from height and weight alone.

Method and limitations

Enter skeletal muscle mass from a suitable assessment, then calculate its percentage of body weight and its height-normalized index (skeletal muscle mass ÷ height²).

The height-normalized value is a descriptive index, not a universally standardized diagnostic measure. Device definitions and methods vary; no clinical cutoff or diagnosis is assigned. Height and weight alone cannot reliably determine skeletal muscle mass.
