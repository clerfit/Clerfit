Understand Skeletal Muscle Mass +

This page accepts skeletal-muscle mass from an assessment and reports its relation to total body mass plus a height-indexed value.

Calculation method

Relative skeletal muscle mass = SMM ÷ body weight × 100. Skeletal-muscle-mass index = SMM in kg ÷ height in metres².

Measurement protocol

Use an assessment that explicitly reports skeletal-muscle mass and record body weight and height from the same period. The calculator handles the selected mass and height units.

Interpretation and limits

There is no reliable universal SMM estimate from height and weight alone. Device algorithms differ, and height-indexed metrics require a suitable age-, sex- and population-matched reference to interpret.
