Fat Intake Calculator

Fat % represents the percentage of your total daily calories that come from fats. Recommended range is 20–35% depending on age, health goals, and activity level.

What is Fat Intake?

Fat intake refers to the amount of dietary fat you should consume daily based on your total energy needs. Fats are essential for hormone production, brain health, vitamin absorption, and long-term energy supply.

Applications of Fat Intake

  • Supports hormone production.
  • Helps absorb vitamins A, D, E, and K.
  • Provides long-lasting energy.
  • Supports brain and nervous system health.
  • Important for growth in children and teens.
  • Helps balance macronutrient distribution.
  • Supports healthy skin and cell function.
