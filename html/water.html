Water Intake

Water intake refers to the amount of water consumed daily through drinking and food to maintain proper hydration. Adequate water intake supports bodily functions such as digestion, circulation, temperature regulation, and overall health.

Water Intake – Applications

  • Maintains proper hydration for all body functions
  • Supports digestion and nutrient absorption
  • Helps regulate body temperature
  • Assists in weight management
  • Essential for kidney and liver health
  • Supports healthy skin and hair
  • Used in fitness and health tracking apps

Related Health Problems

Low Water Intake (Dehydration)

  • Fatigue and weakness
  • Headaches and dizziness
  • Kidney stones and urinary issues
  • Dry skin and poor digestion
  • Reduced physical performance

Excess Water Intake (Overhydration)

  • Electrolyte imbalance
  • Swelling in tissues (edema)
  • Low sodium levels (hyponatremia)
  • Possible kidney strain in rare cases
