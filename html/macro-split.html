About Macro Split

Meaning:

Macro Split divides your daily calories into proteins, carbohydrates, and fats to support your fitness goal, whether it's muscle gain, fat loss, or weight maintenance.

Applications:

  • Optimized nutrition for fat loss or muscle gain
  • Supports recovery and energy levels
  • Used in personalized diet plans
  • Maintains overall health and performance

Health Concerns if Ignored:

  • Muscle loss or inadequate recovery
  • Uncontrolled fat or carb intake leading to weight gain
  • Imbalanced diet affecting metabolism and energy
