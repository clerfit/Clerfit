Method and limitations

For each person: fat-free mass = body weight × (1 − body-fat percentage ÷ 100). FFMI = fat-free mass (kg) ÷ height (m)². The calculator displays both indices and their absolute difference.

Body-fat percentage is an estimate and its error carries into FFMI. Use comparable measurement methods for both people. FFMI does not measure muscle quality, health, strength or athletic potential, and the comparison is not a ranking.
