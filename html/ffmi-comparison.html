Understand FFMI Comparison +

This comparison calculates fat-free mass index for two people and reports their difference. FFMI adjusts estimated fat-free mass for height; it is not a direct muscle measurement.

Calculation method

For each person: fat-free mass = weight × (1 − body-fat fraction); FFMI = fat-free mass ÷ height². Difference = Person B FFMI − Person A FFMI.

Measurement protocol

Enter weight, body-fat percentage and height for each person, using consistent body-fat methods and comparable measurement conditions. Verify that the percentage is entered on a 0–100 scale.

Interpretation and limits

Fat-free mass includes water, organs and bone, not only muscle. The comparison is sensitive to body-fat estimate error and body-size context; no universal health or athletic threshold is assigned.
