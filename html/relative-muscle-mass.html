Method and limitations

Relative muscle mass = measured skeletal muscle mass ÷ body weight × 100. Enter a measured muscle-mass result from a suitable body-composition assessment; this calculator does not estimate muscle mass from height, weight or appearance.

The result is a ratio of the supplied values, not a clinical classification. Different devices and assessment methods may define or estimate skeletal muscle mass differently, so compare results only when the measurement method is consistent.
