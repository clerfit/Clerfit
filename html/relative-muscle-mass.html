Understand Relative Muscle Mass +

Relative muscle mass expresses an assessment-reported skeletal-muscle mass as a share of body weight. This page does not infer muscle from photographs, height or weight.

Calculation method

Relative muscle mass (%) = measured skeletal-muscle mass ÷ body weight × 100 after converting both masses to kilograms.

Measurement protocol

Enter muscle mass and body weight from the same device or assessment. Confirm that the device’s definition of skeletal muscle matches the value being entered.

Interpretation and limits

Device definitions and algorithms vary. A higher percentage alone does not establish strength, health or performance, and the value can change with body weight and measurement conditions.
