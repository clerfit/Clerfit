Protein Intake

Protein intake refers to the amount of protein consumed through food or supplements to meet the body’s daily requirements. Adequate protein intake supports muscle growth, tissue repair, enzyme and hormone production, and overall health.

Protein – Applications

  • Essential for muscle building and repair
  • Supports weight management and fat loss
  • Helps in overall growth and development
  • Used in diet planning for athletes and fitness enthusiasts
  • Maintains healthy skin, hair, and nails
  • Supports immune system function
  • Used in health tracking and nutrition apps

Related Health Problems

Low Protein Intake

  • Muscle loss and weakness
  • Fatigue and low energy
  • Slow recovery from injuries
  • Weakened immunity
  • Hair, skin, and nail problems

Excess Protein Intake

  • Kidney strain in susceptible individuals
  • Dehydration
  • Digestive issues
  • Potential weight gain if calories are excessive
