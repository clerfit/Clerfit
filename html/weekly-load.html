Weekly Load Calculator

Weekly Load represents the total training workload performed in a week.

Weekly Load = Weight × Reps × Sets × Sessions per Week

Meaning & Importance

  • Represents the total workload performed over a week.
  • Helps plan training frequency and recovery.
  • Tracks progression and prevents overtraining.

Applications

  • Design weekly programs for strength, hypertrophy, or endurance.
  • Adjust training frequency and volume according to recovery.
  • Monitor total workload for athletes and fitness enthusiasts.

Reference Table (Weekly Load Guidance)

Weekly Load (kg)Interpretation
< 5000Low – beginners or light sessions
5000 – 15000Moderate – intermediate lifters
15000 – 25000High – advanced lifters
> 25000Very High – professional/athlete level; caution advised

Caution: Ensure proper form and progressive overload. Excessive weekly load can increase injury risk. Adjust based on experience, recovery, and goals.
