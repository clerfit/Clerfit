RMR Calculator (Resting Metabolic Rate)

What is RMR?

Resting Metabolic Rate (RMR) is the number of calories your body burns at rest to maintain vital functions such as breathing, circulation, cell production, and organ function. It represents the minimum energy required to keep your body alive without physical activity.

Applications of RMR

  • Helps determine daily calorie needs.
  • Used to calculate Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE).
  • Supports weight loss and fat loss planning.
  • Useful for muscle gain and bulking programs.
  • Assists dietitians in nutrition planning.
  • Helps track metabolic changes over time.
  • Used in fitness and health monitoring apps.
