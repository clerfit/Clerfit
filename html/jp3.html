Understand Body Fat Jackson-Pollock 3-Site Calculator +

This page estimates adult body-fat percentage from three skinfold sites. The site set changes with the equation group: chest, abdomen and thigh for the male equation; triceps, suprailiac and thigh for the female equation.

Calculation method

The sum of the three skinfolds and age produce a predicted body density using the sex-specific Jackson–Pollock coefficients. The page then applies Siri: body-fat % = 495 ÷ density − 450. It is an empirical prediction equation, not a direct body-fat measurement.

Measurement protocol

Choose the equation group that matches the reference method, enter age, and use the same calibrated caliper and anatomical landmarks described by that protocol. Take skinfolds consistently; repeating readings and using the protocol’s prescribed averaging reduces operator variation.

Interpretation and limits

The implemented equation bands are for adults: male JP3 ages 18–61 and female JP3 ages 18–55. This is not a direct body-fat measurement. The original equations were developed in particular adult samples, so accuracy can differ by population, body-fat range, technique and assessor. Do not compare results from different equations as if they were interchangeable.

Jackson AW, Pollock ML. Generalized equations for predicting body density of men. 1978. British Journal of Nutrition; Jackson, Pollock & Ward. Generalized equations for predicting body density of women. 1980. Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise.
