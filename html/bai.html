Calculate adult BAI

The original BAI method was developed in adults. This calculator requires age 18 or older and does not assign health-risk categories.

Method and limitations

BAI = hip circumference (cm) ÷ height (m)1.5 − 18. The result is expressed as an estimated body-adiposity percentage by the original method.

BAI was developed in adult study populations. A later comparison reported bias by sex and body-fat level and found no prediction advantage over BMI or circumference measures. Treat it as one approximate index, not a direct measurement, diagnosis, or stand-alone health assessment.

Understand Body Adiposity Index (BAI) +

The Body Adiposity Index uses hip circumference and height to calculate an adult estimate. It does not require body weight, which distinguishes it from BMI.

Calculation method

BAI = hip circumference in centimetres ÷ height in metres^1.5 − 18. The original paper interpreted the value as estimated body-adiposity percentage; it remains a prediction, not a direct measurement.

Measurement protocol

Enter hip circumference in centimetres and standing height in the selected height unit. Use a defined hip landmark and the adult age range accepted by the page; avoid interpreting pediatric values.

Interpretation and limits

BAI was developed in specific adult populations. Later evaluation reported bias and no clear advantage over BMI or waist measures for cardiovascular-risk prediction. Do not use the result as a diagnosis or universal cutoff.

Original method: Bergman RN et al. A Better Index of Body Adiposity (2011). Independent comparison: evaluation of BAI for cardiovascular risk (2012).
