Calculate adult BAI

The original BAI method was developed in adults. This calculator requires age 18 or older and does not assign health-risk categories.

Method and limitations

BAI = hip circumference (cm) ÷ height (m)1.5 − 18. The result is expressed as an estimated body-adiposity percentage by the original method.

BAI was developed in adult study populations. A later comparison reported bias by sex and body-fat level and found no prediction advantage over BMI or circumference measures. Treat it as one approximate index, not a direct measurement, diagnosis, or stand-alone health assessment.

How to use it

Enter adult age, hip circumference and height. Select the unit beside each measurement; the calculator converts inputs to canonical units before applying the published BAI equation.

Important: Results depend on measurement technique and the quality of the inputs. They are not a substitute for professional advice.
