Walking Speed Calculator

Calculate your average walking speed based on distance and time.

Formula:

Walking Speed = Distance ÷ Time
Output is in km/h or mi/h depending on distance unit.

Applications:

  • Track walking performance and pace
  • Plan daily walking goals or fitness routines
  • Compare speed over different distances or days
  • Monitor improvements in endurance and mobility

Cautions:

  • Ensure accurate distance measurement for correct speed calculation
  • Do not walk beyond safe limits or ignore physical discomfort
  • Use results as a guideline; individual walking ability may vary
Understand Walking Speed +

What is Walking Speed?

Walking Speed is a pace, speed, distance, time or endurance estimate. The result depends on the inputs and method used by this calculator.

What does the result mean?

Interpret the Walking Speed result in the context of the input values, displayed units and assumptions. Terrain, weather, pacing and measurement accuracy affect results. Calorie and VO₂ estimates are not laboratory measurements.

Calculation methodAverage speed = distance ÷ elapsed time.

The result is an average over the entered interval and may not reflect changes in speed along the route.

Uses and benefits

It can help plan and compare activity when distance and timing are measured consistently.

Limitations and cons

Terrain, weather, pacing and measurement accuracy affect results. Calorie and VO₂ estimates are not laboratory measurements.

How to use it

Enter the measured distance and elapsed time or the activity values requested, then confirm the unit format before calculating.
