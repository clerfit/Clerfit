Method and limitations

Each nutrient is compared separately with its own age-, sex- and life-stage-specific Recommended Dietary Allowance (RDA). Percentages are not combined into an overall nutrition score.

These U.S. Dietary Reference Intake values are intended for healthy populations aged 14 and above. An RDA comparison is not a diagnosis of deficiency, adequacy or toxicity. Individual needs may differ with medical conditions, absorption, medications and clinical circumstances.

Vitamin A total retinol activity equivalents (RAE) are not compared with the UL because the UL applies only to preformed retinol. No UL has been established for vitamin B12. Vitamin B6 and C UL values, where shown, are context only and are not intake targets.

Reference sources: U.S. National Institutes of Health, Office of Dietary Supplements (ODS) health professional fact sheets for vitamins A, B12, B6 and C.
