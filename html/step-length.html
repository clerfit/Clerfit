Step Length Calculator

Estimate your average step length using distance covered and number of steps taken.

Formula:

Step Length = Total Distance ÷ Number of Steps

Meaning:

Step length is the average distance covered in one step. It helps estimate walking efficiency, gait pattern, and stride analysis.

Applications:

  • Walking and running performance analysis
  • Fitness tracking and pedometer accuracy
  • Gait and rehabilitation assessment
  • Sports biomechanics evaluation

Caution:

  • Ensure accurate step count for reliable results.
  • Use consistent walking conditions (flat surface preferred).
  • Average step length differs between walking and running.
