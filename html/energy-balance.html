Energy Balance Calculator

What is Energy Balance?

Energy balance is the relationship between calories consumed (energy in) and calories burned (energy out). Your body weight changes depending on whether you are in a calorie surplus, deficit, or maintenance state.

Applications of Energy Balance

  • Helps understand weight gain or weight loss.
  • Used to track dieting progress.
  • Supports fat loss planning.
  • Helps manage muscle-building phases.
  • Useful for athletes monitoring calorie intake.
  • Prevents unintentional weight changes.
  • Forms the foundation of nutrition science.

What does the result mean?

Interpret the Energy Balance result in the context of the input values, displayed units and assumptions. Equations and activity factors are estimates; actual energy needs vary. Avoid using a single result as an exact prescription.

Calculation methodEstimated energy balance = energy intake − energy expenditure.

Both intake and expenditure may contain substantial estimation error; interpret small differences cautiously.
