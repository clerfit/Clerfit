Understand Chest-to-Waist Ratio Calculator +

This page divides chest circumference by waist circumference to describe two tape measurements on a common scale.

Calculation method

Chest-to-waist ratio = chest circumference ÷ waist circumference. Convert both measurements to the same length unit before division.

Measurement protocol

Use consistent chest and waist landmarks, posture, breathing phase and tape tension. Enter both values in the selected shared unit; do not mix a relaxed and expanded chest reading.

Interpretation and limits

The ratio is a descriptive number and has no universal clinical cutoff. It can shift with posture, respiration and measurement placement and should not be used to infer health or fitness status.
