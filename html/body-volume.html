Understand Body Volume Estimate +

This calculator derives volume from a supplied mass and density. It does not estimate a person's density or measure displacement.

Calculation method

Volume = mass ÷ density. With mass in kilograms and density in kg/L, the result is litres because kg ÷ (kg/L) = L.

Measurement protocol

Use mass and density values that refer to the same object and use compatible units. Ensure density is positive and comes from a measurement or justified method.

Interpretation and limits

The output is only as reliable as the supplied density. Human body composition is not captured by one universal density value, and this arithmetic should not be treated as a body-fat or health estimate.
