Body Surface Area (BSA)

Body Surface Area (BSA) is an estimate of the body's surface area, expressed in square metres (m²). Some clinical protocols use BSA as one input, but dosing and treatment decisions must follow the specific approved protocol and a qualified clinician's instructions.

BSA Applications

  • Selected clinical calculations when a validated protocol specifies BSA
  • Clinical research and body-size indexing
  • Kidney function assessment
  • Cardiac output indexing
  • Burn treatment evaluation
  • Clinical research measurements
  • Pediatric growth assessment

Why BSA is Important

  • Used in selected clinical calculations when a validated protocol specifies BSA
  • Used in intensive care and surgery
  • Important for metabolic and cardiac studies
  • Standard measurement in hospitals worldwide
