Method and limitations

Compare biacromial breadth (distance between the acromion landmarks) with biacristal breadth (distance between the iliac-crest landmarks). Accurate landmarking and a suitable anthropometric caliper are important.

Ratio = first measurement ÷ second measurement. Convert all measurements to centimetres before calculation. Use the same landmarks, posture, tape tension or caliper protocol when comparing results. This descriptive result is not a diagnosis, a validated health-risk score or a measure of attractiveness or athletic potential.
