What is Facial Thirds?

Facial Thirds is a ratio or descriptive comparison of manually measured facial landmarks. The result depends on the inputs and method used by this calculator.

What does the result mean?

Interpret the Facial Thirds result in the context of the input values, displayed units and assumptions. Facial ratios are not universal standards of attractiveness, health or medical status. Camera angle and landmark choice affect measurements.

Uses and benefits

It can help make manual measurements repeatable when the same landmarks and method are used.

Limitations and cons

Facial ratios are not universal standards of attractiveness, health or medical status. Camera angle and landmark choice affect measurements.

How to use it

Follow the page’s landmark instructions, use compatible units, and enter the measurements manually. This tool does not automatically analyse a face photo unless explicitly stated.

Important: ClerFit provides educational calculations. It does not replace professional medical, nutrition or exercise advice. Check the calculator's formula and units before relying on a result.
