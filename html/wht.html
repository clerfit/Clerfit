Waist-to-Height Ratio (WHtR)

Waist-to-Height Ratio measures abdominal fat distribution by comparing waist circumference to height. It complements other screening measures but does not diagnose disease or replace clinical assessment.

Applications

  • Cardiovascular risk screening
  • Metabolic syndrome detection
  • Obesity risk assessment
  • Better predictor than BMI in many cases
  • Use adult screening rules cautiously; pediatric interpretation differs

Health Guideline

For adults, waist circumference below half of height is a commonly used rule of thumb, not a diagnosis or a universal category system.
