What is Calories Burned?

Calories Burned is a pace, speed, distance, time or endurance estimate. The result depends on the inputs and method used by this calculator.

What does the result mean?

Interpret the Calories Burned result in the context of the input values, displayed units and assumptions. Terrain, weather, pacing and measurement accuracy affect results. Calorie and VO₂ estimates are not laboratory measurements.
