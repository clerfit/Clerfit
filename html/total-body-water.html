Understand Total Body Water Percentage Calculator +

This calculator expresses a total-body-water value supplied by an assessment as a percentage of body mass. It does not predict water content from height, sex or weight alone.

Calculation method

Total body water % = assessment-reported total body water (kg) ÷ body weight (kg) × 100. Both mass values must refer to the same person and compatible units.

Measurement protocol

Enter the total-body-water reading and body weight recorded for the same assessment session. Use a device or clinical measurement that explicitly reports total body water; do not substitute a hydration goal or fluid intake.

Interpretation and limits

The percentage describes the supplied measurement, not whether hydration is adequate. Device estimates depend on method, population, recent food, fluid, exercise and other conditions. This output is not a diagnosis of dehydration or fluid overload.
