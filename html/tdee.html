TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure)

Definition

TDEE is the total number of calories a person burns in a day, including all activities and bodily functions. It combines Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) with calories burned through physical activity, exercise, and digestion.

Applications

  • Determines daily caloric needs for maintaining weight
  • Helps plan weight loss or weight gain programs
  • Supports personalized diet and nutrition planning
  • Used by fitness trainers to optimize training and recovery
  • Monitors energy balance for athletic performance
  • Helps prevent underfeeding or overeating
  • Integrated in health and fitness tracking apps

Related Health Problems

Low TDEE (Below Calorie Needs)

  • Weight loss or undernutrition if intake is insufficient
  • Fatigue and low energy
  • Muscle loss
  • Nutrient deficiencies
  • Weakened immunity

High TDEE (Above Calorie Needs)

  • Potential weight gain if caloric intake exceeds expenditure
  • Increased risk of obesity and related diseases
  • May indicate excessive physical activity without adequate nutrition
  • Risk of nutrient imbalances if diet is not adjusted

What is TDEE Calculator?

TDEE Calculator is an estimate of energy use or a calorie target. The result depends on the inputs and method used by this calculator.

What does the result mean?

It provides a starting estimate of daily energy needs, rather than a guaranteed calorie requirement.

Calculation methodA common estimate is TDEE = estimated BMR × activity factor.

Activity factors are approximations. Real energy expenditure varies and is best evaluated alongside longer-term trends.

Uses and benefits

Useful for planning and comparing intake with activity over time.

Limitations and cons

Activity multipliers and equations introduce uncertainty; compare estimates with longer-term trends rather than treating them as exact.

How to use it

Read each field label and its unit, enter your measurements or values, check that none are missing or implausible, and activate the calculator button. Review the displayed result, formula and limitations. If comparing results over time, use the same measurement method.

Important: ClerFit provides educational calculations. It does not replace professional medical, nutrition or exercise advice. Check the calculator's formula and units before relying on a result.
