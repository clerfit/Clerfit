What Are Daily Energy Needs?

Daily energy needs represent the total number of calories your body requires each day to support basic bodily functions and physical activity. It includes your Resting Metabolic Rate (RMR) plus calories burned through movement and exercise.

Applications of Daily Energy Needs

  • Helps maintain healthy body weight.
  • Used to design weight loss or muscle gain plans.
  • Supports balanced diet planning.
  • Useful for athletes optimizing performance.
  • Prevents overeating or undereating.
  • Guides personalized nutrition programs.
  • Forms the foundation of energy balance tracking.
