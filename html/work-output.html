Work Output Calculator

Calculate the total mechanical work done during lifting based on weight, distance moved, and repetitions.

Formula:

Work Output (J) = Weight × Gravity × Distance × Reps
(Weight in kg, Distance in meters, Reps count, Gravity = 9.81 m/s²)

Applications:

  • Assess total mechanical work in strength training
  • Track progress in lifting performance
  • Compare work output across different training sessions
  • Use for programming and load monitoring

Cautions:

  • Always warm up before lifting heavy weights
  • Maintain proper lifting form to prevent injury
  • Do not exceed your lifting capacity without supervision
  • Ratios and totals are guidelines; individual variation applies
