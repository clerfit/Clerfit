BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate)

Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) is the number of calories your body needs at rest to maintain basic physiological functions such as breathing, circulation, and cell production. It represents the minimum energy required to keep the body alive while at rest.

BMR Applications

  • Calculates daily calorie needs at rest
  • Helps plan weight loss or weight gain diets
  • Used by nutritionists for meal planning
  • Supports fitness and bodybuilding programs
  • Helps track metabolic health
  • Useful in medical and health assessments
  • Base input for calorie-tracking apps

Related Health Problems

Low BMR

  • Easy weight gain
  • Low energy and fatigue
  • Cold sensitivity
  • Possible thyroid issues

High BMR

  • Difficulty gaining weight
  • Rapid calorie burn
  • Increased hunger
  • Risk of muscle loss if underfed

What is BMR Calculator?

BMR Calculator is an estimate of energy use or a calorie target. The result depends on the inputs and method used by this calculator.

What does the result mean?

It is a resting-energy estimate, not the full amount of energy needed across a normal day.

Calculation methodIf using Mifflin–St Jeor: male equation = 10 × mass(kg) + 6.25 × height(cm) − 5 × age + 5; female equation uses −161 instead of +5.

Different BMR equations can produce different estimates. Confirm the method selected by this page and do not treat BMR as total daily energy expenditure.

Uses and benefits

Useful as one input when estimating total daily energy needs.

Limitations and cons

Equation-based results are estimates and can differ from measured metabolic rate; BMR is not the same as TDEE.

How to use it

Read each field label and its unit, enter your measurements or values, check that none are missing or implausible, and activate the calculator button. Review the displayed result, formula and limitations. If comparing results over time, use the same measurement method.
