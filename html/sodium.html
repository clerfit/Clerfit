Sodium Intake Reference

Estimate the age-based adequate-intake reference for sodium. This is not an individualized prescription. Sweat losses, medical conditions and medications can change individual needs.

For ages 14 and older, 1,500 mg/day is the adequate-intake reference; 2,300 mg/day is a general adult limit used in dietary guidance, not a target everyone should aim to consume. For medical conditions or unusually heavy sweating, seek individualized advice.
