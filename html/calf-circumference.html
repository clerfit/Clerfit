Calculate calf geometry

Measure the largest calf girth with the same posture, side and tape tension each time. The circular model is a geometric approximation only.

Leave segment length blank if you only need the circle-equivalent cross-sectional area.

Method and limitations

For circumference C, diameter = C ÷ π, radius = C ÷ (2π), and circle-equivalent area = C² ÷ (4π). If segment length L is entered, the cylindrical volume proxy is area × L.

The optional volume assumes a constant circular cross-section along the entire segment. Real calf shape and tissue distribution vary; the output is not muscle volume, body composition, or a clinical assessment.

Understand Calf Circumference +

This calculator derives circle-equivalent calf dimensions from circumference and optionally multiplies area by a segment length to form a cylindrical volume proxy.

Calculation method

For circumference C, area = C² ÷ (4π), diameter = C ÷ π, and radius = C ÷ (2π). Optional volume proxy = area × segment length.

Measurement protocol

Measure the calf at a chosen, reproducible site and enter any segment length for that same region. Use matching length units and repeat the same posture and tape tension.

Interpretation and limits

A calf is not a constant circular cylinder. These values do not measure muscle cross-section, diagnose sarcopenia or estimate total leg volume; they illustrate idealized geometry only.
