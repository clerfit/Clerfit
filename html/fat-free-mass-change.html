Understand Fat-Free Mass Change Calculator +

This calculator estimates fat-free mass at two time points from body weight and body-fat percentage, then subtracts the earlier estimate from the current one.

Calculation method

Fat-free mass = body weight × (1 − body-fat percentage ÷ 100). Change = current fat-free mass − earlier fat-free mass. Both weights are entered in kilograms.

Measurement protocol

For each date, enter body weight and body-fat percentage from measurements taken under similar conditions. Use the same body-fat method for both dates and retain the source readings.

Interpretation and limits

Fat-free mass includes water, bone and organs as well as muscle. Errors in body fat and weight carry into both estimates; the result does not show that skeletal muscle changed by the same amount.
