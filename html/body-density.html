Understand Body Density +

Body density is mass divided by volume for measurements of the same object. This calculator returns the quotient with compatible units.

Calculation method

Density = mass ÷ volume. For kilograms and litres the output unit is kg/L; for grams and millilitres it is g/mL.

Measurement protocol

Enter mass and volume from the same measurement context and convert units consistently before comparison. Volume must be positive.

Interpretation and limits

Density is not body-fat percentage. Converting body density to body fat requires additional assumptions and a specific population-appropriate equation; this page performs no such conversion.
