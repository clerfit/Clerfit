Understand YMCA Body Fat Calculator +

The YMCA method uses waist circumference and body weight to calculate an estimated body-fat percentage. Use the same waist landmark and measurement conditions when comparing readings.

Calculation method

The implemented equation converts weight to pounds and waist to inches, then calculates ((4.15 × waist − 0.082 × weight − constant) ÷ weight) × 100. The constant is 98.42 for the male equation and 76.76 for the female equation.

Measurement protocol

Use the same waist landmark and tape tension for repeat readings. Enter body weight and waist circumference as measured; the calculator handles unit conversion internally. Check that the resulting estimate is plausible for the inputs.

Interpretation and limits

Results can vary with measurement technique and individual differences. Measure consistently and interpret the result alongside other relevant health and fitness information.
