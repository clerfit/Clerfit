Ponderal Index (PI)

Definition

The Ponderal Index (PI) is a measure of the leanness or corpulence of a person, calculated using height and weight. It is defined as weight (kg) divided by height (m) cubed: PI = weight / height³. It is another height-adjusted index; it should not be assumed superior to BMI for all people or uses.

Applications

  • Provides a different height-adjusted index for descriptive comparison
  • Used in pediatric and adult growth studies
  • Helps evaluate nutritional status and risk of obesity
  • Supports fitness and health assessment programs
  • Used in research for body composition and health risk studies
  • Useful for comparing body types across different populations

Limitations

A Ponderal Index value alone cannot diagnose undernutrition, obesity or associated diseases. Use age- and population-appropriate references when interpretation is needed.
