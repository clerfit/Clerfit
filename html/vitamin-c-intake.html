Calculator Guide +

How to measure

Estimate vitamin C from a typical day's food, fortified foods and supplements using package labels or a reliable nutrition tracker. Match the serving size you actually consumed. Enter mg, or select mcg if that is the unit on your source. This is an intake estimate, not a blood test.

How to calculate

  1. Choose age and reference sex.
  2. Select pregnancy or lactation if applicable; those references are available for female profiles aged 14–50.
  3. Enter the day's vitamin C total and choose mg or mcg.
  4. Calculate. Micrograms are converted to milligrams before comparison.
FormulaRDA percentage = daily vitamin C intake (mg) ÷ selected vitamin C RDA (mg) × 100

If entered in mcg: mg = mcg ÷ 1,000. The RDA depends on age, reference sex and eligible life stage.

Understanding the result

The percentage compares your estimated daily intake with the selected U.S. RDA. It is a reference comparison, not a diagnosis or a complete assessment of your diet.

Limitations

Food records and labels are estimates, and intake can vary from day to day. The U.S. UL is context only, not an intake target or diagnosis. Reference values are for healthy populations aged 14 and above and may not reflect individual medical circumstances.

Method and limitations

Percentage of RDA = entered daily intake ÷ selected age-, sex- and life-stage-specific RDA × 100.

These U.S. Dietary Reference Intake values are intended for healthy populations aged 14 and above. An RDA comparison is not a diagnosis of deficiency, adequacy or toxicity. Individual needs may differ with medical conditions, absorption, medications and clinical circumstances.

Vitamin A total retinol activity equivalents (RAE) are not compared with the UL because the UL applies only to preformed retinol. No UL has been established for vitamin B12. Vitamin B6 and C UL values, where shown, are context only and are not intake targets.

Reference sources: U.S. National Institutes of Health, Office of Dietary Supplements (ODS) health professional fact sheets for vitamins A, B12, B6 and C.
