Squat Ratio Calculator

The Squat-to-Bodyweight Ratio measures lower body strength relative to body weight.

Formula:

Squat Ratio = Squat Weight ÷ Body Weight
(Both weights must be in the same unit: kg or lbs)

Applications:

  • Assess lower body strength relative to body weight
  • Track progress in strength training programs
  • Compare strength across athletes of different body sizes
  • Set training goals based on strength category
  • Helps in sports performance evaluation

Squat Ratio Categories:

Squat RatioCategoryMeaning
< 1.0BeginnerBasic strength, new lifter
1.0 – 1.49IntermediateGood strength, can handle moderate loads
1.5 – 1.99AdvancedStrong, experienced lifter
≥ 2.0EliteExceptional strength, highly trained

Cautions:

  • Always warm up properly before attempting heavy squats
  • Maintain proper squat form to avoid knee, hip, or back injuries
  • Do not lift beyond your capacity without supervision
  • Consult a fitness professional if unsure about technique
  • Use ratios as a guideline; individual variation applies
