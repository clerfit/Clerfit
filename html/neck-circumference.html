Measure at the same defined neck landmark each time, with the tape level and not compressing the skin. Choose the unit for this calculator only.

Method and limitations

For circumference C, the equivalent circular diameter is C ÷ π, radius is C ÷ (2π), and cross-sectional area is C² ÷ (4π).

The human neck is not a perfect circle. These values are geometric approximations only; they do not estimate airway size, tissue composition, body fat, or medical risk.

Understand Neck Circumference +

This page converts neck circumference into the diameter, radius and cross-sectional area of a circle with the same perimeter. It is a geometry exercise, not an anatomical scan.

Calculation method

For circumference C: equivalent diameter = C ÷ π; radius = C ÷ (2π); area = C² ÷ (4π).

Measurement protocol

Measure around the neck at the site specified by your chosen protocol, without compressing soft tissue. Select the matching unit and enter the circumference only once.

Interpretation and limits

A human neck is not circular and changes shape along its length. These equivalent-circle values do not estimate airway size, fat, muscle, or medical risk.
