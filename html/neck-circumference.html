Method and limitations

For circumference C, the equivalent circular diameter is C ÷ π, radius is C ÷ (2π), and cross-sectional area is C² ÷ (4π).

The human neck is not a perfect circle. These values are geometric approximations only; they do not estimate airway size, tissue composition, body fat, or medical risk.

How to use it

Measure the neck at a consistent landmark with the tape level and gently in contact with the skin. Enter the circumference and select Calculate to see the circle-equivalent geometry.

Important: Results depend on measurement technique and the quality of the inputs. They are not a substitute for professional advice.
