Pull-Up Index Calculator

The Pull-Up Index evaluates your upper body relative strength based on body weight.

Pull-Up Index = Number of Pull-Ups ÷ Body Weight

Meaning & Importance

  • Shows your relative upper body strength.
  • Helps assess performance potential in climbing, calisthenics, and gymnastics.
  • Tracks strength progress over time in a bodyweight context.

Applications

  • Training programs focused on pull-ups, bodyweight exercises, or climbing.
  • Setting personalized pull-up goals.
  • Comparing relative strength across individuals.
