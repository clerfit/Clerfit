Understand Body Fat Change Calculator +

This calculator reports how a body-fat percentage reading differs from an earlier reading. It distinguishes percentage points from a relative percent change.

Calculation method

Change in percentage points = current body-fat % − earlier body-fat %. Relative change = difference ÷ |earlier value| × 100 when the earlier value is non-zero.

Measurement protocol

Enter readings from the same assessment method and use the same reporting convention. Keep measurement conditions similar; the calculator does not identify the method or correct its bias.

Interpretation and limits

An apparent change may fall within measurement error. Percentage-point change is not a kilogram change, and results from skinfold, impedance and imaging methods are not interchangeable.
