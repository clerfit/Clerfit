What is Physical Activity Level?

PAL = average total energy expenditure (TEE) over 24 hours ÷ basal metabolic rate (BMR) over 24 hours. Both inputs must use the same energy unit and time basis. This calculator computes the ratio from the two values you supply; it does not estimate TEE or BMR for you.

Limitations

FAO/WHO/UNU classifies adult population lifestyles as 1.40–1.69 sedentary/light, 1.70–1.99 active/moderate and 2.00–2.40 vigorous. These are population reference bands, not precise personal cut-offs. Estimated TEE or BMR introduces uncertainty; children, pregnancy and lactation require separate assessment.

Uses and benefits

It can help structure planning and make assumptions visible.

Limitations and cons

Real-world conditions vary; an estimate cannot guarantee safety, health outcomes or exact activity results.

How to use it

Enter your average 24-hour TEE and BMR in kcal/day, using measured values or clearly identified estimates from the same period. Select Calculate PAL. For example, 2,970 kcal/day ÷ 1,700 kcal/day gives a PAL of 1.75. Do not enter a workout multiplier in place of TEE or BMR.

Important: ClerFit provides educational calculations. It does not replace professional medical, nutrition or exercise advice. Check the calculator's formula and units before relying on a result.
