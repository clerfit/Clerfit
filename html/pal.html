What this tool does

  • It selects a conventional activity multiplier that is sometimes used with a separate resting-energy estimate to form a rough total daily energy expenditure (TDEE) estimate.
  • Formal physical activity level (PAL) is defined as total energy expenditure over 24 hours divided by basal metabolic rate over 24 hours. This page does not calculate or measure that ratio.
  • The selected value is an assumption, not a personalized or scientifically measured activity score. The result is not a calorie target.

Possible use

  • Use the factor only as a starting assumption in a separate TDEE estimate.
  • Review estimates against several weeks of observed trends and relevant professional guidance instead of treating them as exact requirements.

Limitations and cautions

  • Activity patterns are illustrative and are not universally standardized definitions.
  • This tool does not adjust for age, growth, pregnancy, illness, medication, or individual energy-expenditure differences.
  • Do not use the factor alone to prescribe calorie intake or make a medical or nutrition decision.
Understand TDEE activity factors +

What is this factor?

This page selects a conventional activity multiplier sometimes used to estimate TDEE from a separate resting-energy estimate. Formal PAL is the 24-hour total energy expenditure divided by 24-hour basal metabolic rate; this calculator does not compute that ratio.

What does the result mean?

The selected multiplier is a starting assumption, not a measured activity score or personalized energy requirement. Real-world energy expenditure varies; the factor alone cannot provide a safe or exact calorie target.

Uses and benefits

It can help structure planning and make assumptions visible.

Limitations and cons

Real-world conditions vary; an estimate cannot guarantee safety, health outcomes or exact activity results.

How to use it

Choose the activity pattern that most closely describes your usual routine, while considering daily movement and occupational activity. Treat the factor only as a rough starting assumption, then validate any separate TDEE estimate against observed trends.

Important: ClerFit provides educational calculations. It does not replace professional medical, nutrition or exercise advice. Check the calculator's formula and units before relying on a result.
