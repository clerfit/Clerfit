Meaning

  • PAL (Physical Activity Level) represents your overall daily activity level.
  • It is the ratio of total daily energy expenditure (TDEE) to basal metabolic rate (BMR).
  • Higher PAL values indicate a more active lifestyle.

Applications

  • Used to calculate daily calorie needs accurately.
  • Helps plan diet for weight loss, maintenance, or muscle gain.
  • Useful for athletes, fitness tracking, and lifestyle analysis.
  • Supports better understanding of energy balance.

Cautions

  • PAL is an estimate and may vary based on actual daily activity.
  • Overestimating activity can lead to excess calorie intake.
  • Does not account for metabolic differences between individuals.
  • Should be used along with real progress tracking (weight, performance).
  • Not a substitute for professional medical or dietary advice.
Understand Physical Activity Level +

What is Physical Activity Level?

Physical Activity Level (PAL) is a ratio of estimated total daily energy expenditure to basal or resting energy expenditure. This calculator presents a factor associated with the selected activity category; it does not measure your actual energy expenditure.

What does the result mean?

Interpret the Physical Activity Level result in the context of the input values, displayed units and assumptions. Real-world conditions vary; an estimate cannot guarantee safety, health outcomes or exact activity results.

Uses and benefits

It can help structure planning and make assumptions visible.

Limitations and cons

Real-world conditions vary; an estimate cannot guarantee safety, health outcomes or exact activity results.

How to use it

Enter accurate values and the relevant time period or conditions. Review warnings and do not treat a calculated exposure duration as a safety guarantee.

Important: ClerFit provides educational calculations. It does not replace professional medical, nutrition or exercise advice. Check the calculator's formula and units before relying on a result.
