What is Fiber Intake?

Fiber Intake is an estimate or planning aid for a nutrition or fluid target. The result depends on the inputs and method used by this calculator.

Reference method

This estimate uses age- and sex-specific adequate-intake values or 14 g per 1,000 kcal, whichever is higher. Source: National Academies Dietary Reference Intakes. Activity level is not used as an arbitrary multiplier.

What does the result mean?

Interpret the Fiber Intake result in the context of the input values, displayed units and assumptions. Individual requirements vary with age, activity, climate, diet and health. This tool does not replace individualized clinical advice.

Uses and benefits

It can help organize daily intake and compare a plan with a chosen target.

Limitations and cons

Individual requirements vary with age, activity, climate, diet and health. This tool does not replace individualized clinical advice.

How to use it

Enter the requested values and goals, check the displayed units and assumptions, then use the result as a guide rather than a prescription.

Important: ClerFit provides educational calculations. It does not replace professional medical, nutrition or exercise advice. Check the calculator's formula and units before relying on a result.
