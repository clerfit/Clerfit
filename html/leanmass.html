Lean Body Mass Calculator

Lean mass includes muscle, bone, organs and body water. There is no universal healthy lean-mass percentage cutoff for all ages, sexes and body types; this calculator reports the calculated mass without assigning a category.

Lean Mass

Lean Mass refers to the total weight of the body minus the fat mass, including muscles, bones, organs, and water. It is an important measure for assessing strength, fitness, and overall body composition.

Lean Mass – Applications

  • Helps assess muscle mass vs fat mass
  • Used in strength training and bodybuilding programs
  • Supports weight management and fat loss planning
  • Monitors growth and fitness progress
  • Used by nutritionists to design high-protein diets
  • Tracks metabolic health and overall fitness
  • Useful in health and fitness tracking apps

Related Health Problems

Low Lean Mass

  • Reduced strength and endurance
  • Higher risk of injury
  • Slower metabolism
  • Fatigue and low energy
  • Difficulty maintaining healthy weight

Excess Lean Mass (Rare)

  • May cause joint strain if not balanced with flexibility
  • Possible overtraining injuries
  • Higher calorie requirements to maintain muscle
