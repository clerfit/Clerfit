FFMI (Fat-Free Mass Index)

Fat-Free Mass Index (FFMI) is a measure of the amount of muscle mass relative to height. It is used to assess muscularity and lean body mass, providing a different height-adjusted estimate; it does not directly measure strength or overall fitness.

FFMI (Fat-Free Mass Index) – Applications

  • Measures muscle mass relative to height
  • Used by athletes and bodybuilders to track muscle development
  • Helps design strength training programs
  • Monitors lean body mass progress over time
  • Assists in nutrition planning for muscle gain
  • Supports fitness assessments beyond BMI
  • Useful in health and body composition tracking apps

Related Health Problems

Low FFMI

  • Low muscle mass and strength
  • Slower metabolism
  • Higher risk of injury
  • Difficulty maintaining healthy weight
  • Reduced athletic performance

High FFMI

  • Increased calorie needs to maintain muscle
  • Potential joint strain if not balanced with flexibility
  • Overtraining risks in extreme cases
