Understand Waist Circumference Reference +

This tool records a waist circumference so it can be compared with a chosen external protocol. It does not select a universal threshold or assign a risk tier.

Calculation method

The output is the entered waist circumference with unit conversion where available. Risk interpretation is intentionally not calculated because standards use different measurement sites and reference groups.

Measurement protocol

Measure at the anatomical landmark required by the guideline or study you plan to use. Record posture, breathing phase and tape tension; note the protocol alongside the value.

Interpretation and limits

A waist value is meaningful only with its measurement site and the relevant population guidance. Different organizations use different thresholds; this page does not diagnose abdominal obesity or disease.
