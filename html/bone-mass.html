Understand Bone Mass Estimate Calculator +

This page calculates the proportion of body mass represented by a bone-mass value reported by an assessment. It does not estimate bone mass from height or weight.

Calculation method

Reported bone-mass percentage = assessment-reported bone mass (kg) ÷ body weight (kg) × 100. The output is a proportion, not bone mineral density.

Measurement protocol

Use a bone-mass value from the named device or report and body weight from the same assessment. Do not enter a bone-density score or a guessed skeletal weight.

Interpretation and limits

Bone mass, bone mineral content and bone mineral density are different quantities. This percentage cannot diagnose osteoporosis, fracture risk or bone strength; those require appropriate clinical assessment and validated measurements.
