Carbohydrate Intake Calculator

Carbohydrate % represents the percentage of your total daily calories that come from carbohydrates. Recommended range is 45–65% depending on age, activity level, and fitness goals.

What is Carbohydrate Intake?

Carbohydrate intake represents the amount of carbs required daily based on total energy needs. Carbohydrates are the primary fuel source for the brain, muscles, and growing bodies in children and teenagers.

Applications of Carbohydrate Intake

  • Supports growth and development in children.
  • Provides energy for school and sports activities.
  • Optimizes athletic performance.
  • Supports muscle recovery.
  • Prevents fatigue and low energy.
  • Helps maintain healthy body weight.
  • Balances macronutrient distribution.
