Ideal Weight

Ideal Weight is the optimal weight for a person based on their height, age, gender, and body composition. It is used as a reference to maintain health, reduce the risk of disease, and achieve overall physical fitness.

Ideal Weight – Applications

  • Determines a healthy weight range for height and age
  • Helps set realistic weight goals
  • Used in fitness and wellness planning
  • Supports diet and nutrition programs
  • Used by doctors for basic health assessment
  • Helps monitor growth in adolescents
  • Useful for health and fitness calculators

Related Health Problems

Below Ideal Weight

  • Weak immunity
  • Nutritional deficiencies
  • Fatigue and poor stamina
  • Delayed growth and development

Above Ideal Weight

  • Increased risk of heart disease
  • High blood pressure
  • Type 2 diabetes
  • Joint pain and mobility issues
  • Breathing problems
