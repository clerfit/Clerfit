Heart Rate

Heart Rate is the number of times the heart beats per minute (BPM). It is an important indicator of cardiovascular health, fitness level, and overall physical condition.

Heart Rate – Applications

  • Monitors cardiovascular health and fitness
  • Helps determine training intensity for workouts
  • Used to track resting, maximum, and recovery heart rates
  • Assists in designing safe exercise programs
  • Used by doctors to detect abnormal heart conditions
  • Supports endurance and performance optimization
  • Useful in health and fitness tracking apps

Related Health Problems

Low Heart Rate (Bradycardia)

  • Dizziness or fatigue
  • Low blood pressure
  • Risk of fainting or heart failure in severe cases
  • May indicate underlying heart conditions

High Heart Rate (Tachycardia)

  • Increased risk of heart attack or stroke
  • Palpitations or irregular heartbeat
  • Shortness of breath and chest pain
  • May indicate stress, dehydration, or heart problems
