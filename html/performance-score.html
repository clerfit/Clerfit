Performance Score Calculator

Calculate an overall performance score based on work output and relative strength.

Formula:

Strength Score = Squat Weight ÷ Body Weight
Performance Score = Work Output (kJ) × Strength Score

Applications:

  • Evaluate overall training performance
  • Track improvements over multiple workouts
  • Compare performance across athletes
  • Design progressive training programs

Cautions:

  • Ensure accurate inputs; incorrect work output or squat/body weight will skew results
  • Do not over-rely on the score; individual variation is important
  • Use alongside proper technique and safety measures
