What is Face Shape?

Face Shape is a ratio or descriptive comparison of manually measured facial landmarks. The result depends on the inputs and method used by this calculator.

What does the result mean?

The result is an approximate geometry-based classification, not a judgement of attractiveness or health.

Uses and benefits

Can help organize styling preferences such as glasses or hairstyles.

Limitations and cons

Measurement technique affects the result; faces do not always fit one category, and the tool does not perform automatic photo analysis.

How to use it

Measure each requested landmark carefully using the same unit throughout, enter the values, and select Calculate. Use the diagram as a manual guide only; the page does not automatically analyse a face photo.

Important: ClerFit provides educational calculations. It does not replace professional medical, nutrition or exercise advice. Check the calculator's formula and units before relying on a result.
