Maintenance Calories Calculator (TDEE)

What are Maintenance Calories?

Maintenance calories represent the number of calories your body needs each day to maintain your current weight. It includes energy burned at rest (RMR) plus calories burned through physical activity.

Applications of Maintenance Calories

  • Helps maintain current body weight.
  • Forms the base for weight loss (calorie deficit).
  • Used for muscle gain (calorie surplus planning).
  • Supports diet and meal planning.
  • Used in fitness coaching programs.
  • Helps track metabolic and activity changes.
  • Essential for long-term weight management.
