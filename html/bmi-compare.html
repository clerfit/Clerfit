BMI Comparison

Meaning

BMI (Body Mass Index) estimates whether body weight is appropriate for a person's height.

Applications

  • Fitness and health monitoring
  • Weight-management tracking
  • General obesity screening
  • Comparing body weight status between individuals

Caution

BMI does not directly measure body fat, muscle mass, bone density, or overall health condition. Athletes and muscular individuals may naturally have higher BMI values.
