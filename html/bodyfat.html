Body Fat Percentage Calculator

Body Fat

Body fat refers to the total amount of fat tissue in the body, including essential fat needed for normal physiological functions and storage fat accumulated in adipose tissue. It is an important measure of health and body composition.

Body Fat Percentage – Applications

  • Measures actual fat content in the body
  • Helps assess fitness and athletic performance
  • Used in weight-loss and fat-loss programs
  • Tracks progress in gym and workout routines
  • Better health indicator than BMI in many cases
  • Used by trainers and sports professionals
  • Helps plan diet and calorie intake accurately

Related Health Problems

Low Body Fat

  • Hormonal imbalance
  • Weak immunity
  • Fatigue and low energy
  • Fertility and growth issues

High Body Fat

  • Obesity and overweight
  • Heart disease
  • Type 2 diabetes
  • High blood pressure
  • Joint and mobility problems
