Understand Intracellular Water Calculator +

Intracellular water is water assigned to the inside-cell compartment by a body-composition assessment. This tool reports its share of measured total body water and its share of body mass.

Calculation method

ICW as % of total water = measured ICW ÷ measured TBW × 100. ICW as % of body mass = measured ICW ÷ body weight × 100. The input must come from a method that reports both water compartments.

Measurement protocol

Use intracellular water, total body water and body weight from the same assessment and time point. Check the device’s units before entry; the calculator expects compatible mass values.

Interpretation and limits

Compartment estimates are method-dependent and should not be interpreted as a stand-alone cell-health or hydration diagnosis. ICW cannot exceed TBW in the calculator; different devices may define or estimate compartments differently.
