Method and limitations

Calculate arm span minus standing height. Positive values mean arm span is longer; negative values mean standing height is greater. Small differences can reflect measurement error and technique.

Arm span difference = arm span − standing height. Convert all measurements to centimetres before calculation. Use the same landmarks, posture, tape tension or caliper protocol when comparing results. This descriptive result is not a diagnosis, a validated health-risk score or a measure of attractiveness or athletic potential.
