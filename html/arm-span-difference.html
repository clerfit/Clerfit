This descriptive result is not a diagnosis, a validated health-risk score or a measure of attractiveness or athletic potential.

Understand Arm Span Difference +

This page calculates the signed difference between arm span and standing height, plus a relative expression against height.

Calculation method

Difference = arm span − standing height. Positive means span is larger; negative means height is larger. Relative difference (%) = difference ÷ height × 100.

Measurement protocol

Measure standing height barefoot with a stadiometer and arm span with arms level and fully extended. Use the same units and repeat technique for both.

Interpretation and limits

A small difference may be within measurement error and varies across individuals. The result is not a diagnosis or an indicator of a specific condition by itself.
