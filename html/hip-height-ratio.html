Understand Hip-to-Height Ratio Calculator +

Hip-to-height ratio compares hip circumference with standing height as a descriptive proportion.

Calculation method

Ratio = hip circumference ÷ standing height after unit normalization. The ratio is dimensionless and can be multiplied by 100 for a percentage expression.

Measurement protocol

Use a repeatable hip landmark and a consistent height technique. Select a common unit so circumference and height are comparable.

Interpretation and limits

This measure does not have a universal risk threshold in the calculator. It is different from waist-to-height ratio, which uses waist circumference and has its own screening evidence and protocols.
