Understand Appendicular Lean Mass Index (ALMI) +

Appendicular lean mass index divides lean mass in the arms and legs by height squared. It describes an assessment value relative to stature.

Calculation method

ALMI = appendicular lean mass (kg) ÷ height (m)². Convert mass and height to canonical units before division.

Measurement protocol

Enter appendicular lean mass from a report that defines the measured appendicular regions and standing height from a consistent protocol. Do not substitute total lean mass or skeletal-muscle mass.

Interpretation and limits

ALM and skeletal-muscle mass are not interchangeable. The index alone does not diagnose low muscle mass; clinical cutoffs depend on the measurement method and reference population.
