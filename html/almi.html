Use the sum of arm and leg lean mass from an appropriate assessment, not total lean mass.

Method and limitations

ALMI = appendicular lean mass (kg) ÷ height (m)². Appendicular lean mass is the combined lean mass of the arms and legs, typically obtained from a DXA or suitable body-composition assessment.

Do not substitute total lean body mass for appendicular lean mass. Interpretation depends on the measurement method, sex and reference population. Some clinical frameworks use ALMI thresholds, but this calculator does not apply a diagnosis or universal cutoff.
