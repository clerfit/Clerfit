BMI (Body Mass Index)

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a numerical value calculated from a person’s weight and height, used to categorize underweight, normal weight, overweight, and obesity. It provides a simple assessment of body fatness for population-level health evaluation.

BMI Applications

  • Used to assess overall body fitness
  • Helps classify underweight, normal, overweight, and obesity
  • Used in hospitals for preliminary health screening
  • Helpful for diet and nutrition planning
  • Used by gyms and fitness trainers
  • Supports health monitoring in fitness apps
  • Useful for school and college health projects

Related Health Problems

Underweight

  • Weak immunity
  • Nutritional deficiencies
  • Fatigue and low energy

Overweight

  • High blood pressure
  • Risk of type 2 diabetes
  • Heart-related stress

Obesity

  • Heart disease
  • Diabetes
  • Joint and back pain
  • Breathing and sleep problems
Understand BMI Calculator +

What is BMI Calculator?

BMI Calculator is a body measurement or composition indicator. The result depends on the inputs and method used by this calculator.

Common adult formulaBMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)²

Adult interpretation differs from BMI-for-age assessment for children and teenagers.

What does the result mean?

It gives a quick height-adjusted weight screening value; it does not directly measure body fat or diagnose a condition.

Uses and benefits

Can be calculated with simple measurements and used to follow broad trends.

Limitations and cons

It cannot distinguish fat from muscle, does not describe fat distribution, and adult cut-offs are not appropriate for children and teenagers; use BMI-for-age guidance for under-18s.

How to use it

Read each field label and its unit, enter your measurements or values, check that none are missing or implausible, and activate the calculator button. Review the displayed result, formula and limitations. If comparing results over time, use the same measurement method.

Important: ClerFit provides educational calculations. It does not replace professional medical, nutrition or exercise advice. Check the calculator's formula and units before relying on a result.
