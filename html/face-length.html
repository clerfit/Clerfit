Face Length Ratio Calculator

About Face Length Ratio

The face length ratio compares the lower third of your face (base of nose to chin) with total face length (hairline to chin). It helps in aesthetic analysis and facial proportionality.

Reference from BMI

BMI reflects body composition, and while it does not directly affect face length ratio, extreme BMI levels may influence facial fat distribution, which can slightly alter perceived facial ratios.

What is Face Length Ratio?

Face Length Ratio is a ratio or descriptive comparison of manually measured facial landmarks. The result depends on the inputs and method used by this calculator.

What does the result mean?

Interpret the Face Length Ratio result in the context of the input values, displayed units and assumptions. Facial ratios are not universal standards of attractiveness, health or medical status. Camera angle and landmark choice affect measurements.

Uses and benefits

It can help make manual measurements repeatable when the same landmarks and method are used.

Limitations and cons

Facial ratios are not universal standards of attractiveness, health or medical status. Camera angle and landmark choice affect measurements.

How to use it

Follow the page’s landmark instructions, use compatible units, and enter the measurements manually. This tool does not automatically analyse a face photo unless explicitly stated.
