Understand Upper-Arm-to-Height Ratio Calculator +

This ratio compares upper-arm circumference with standing height. It is a simple proportional measure, not an estimate of muscle size.

Calculation method

Upper-arm-to-height ratio = upper-arm circumference ÷ height after converting to matching units.

Measurement protocol

Use one defined upper-arm site, such as the protocol you are following, and measure standing height consistently. Apply the same tape tension and unit choice for repeat values.

Interpretation and limits

No universal cutoff or health interpretation is assigned. Circumference includes multiple tissues and bone; the ratio cannot distinguish muscle from fat or fluid.
