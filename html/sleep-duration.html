Understand Sleep Duration +

What is Sleep Duration?

Sleep Duration is a descriptive recovery, sleep or training-load metric. The result depends on the inputs and method used by this calculator.

What does the result mean?

Interpret the Sleep Duration result in the context of the input values, displayed units and assumptions. Many recovery indices are planning aids rather than clinically validated measures. Pain, illness and persistent fatigue require appropriate attention.

Calculation methodSleep duration is the elapsed time between sleep and wake times or the duration entered.

Time crossing midnight must be handled correctly; this is not an assessment of sleep quality.

Uses and benefits

It can help organize routines and compare patterns across days or weeks.

Limitations and cons

Many recovery indices are planning aids rather than clinically validated measures. Pain, illness and persistent fatigue require appropriate attention.

How to use it

Enter values for the same time period and measurement convention, then interpret the result alongside how you feel and your training context.
