Understand Arm-to-Waist Ratio +

Arm-to-waist ratio compares arm circumference with waist circumference. It is a descriptive proportion from two tape measurements.

Calculation method

Arm-to-waist ratio = arm circumference ÷ waist circumference. Matching units cancel, so the result is dimensionless.

Measurement protocol

Use a defined arm site and a consistent waist landmark. Enter both measurements in the same selected unit and repeat the protocol for future comparisons.

Interpretation and limits

This is not a validated standalone health-risk score and no universal threshold is assigned. Circumference cannot distinguish muscle, fat, bone or fluid.
