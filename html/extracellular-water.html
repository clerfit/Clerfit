Understand Extracellular Water Calculator +

Extracellular water is the assessment-assigned water outside cells. The calculator expresses the supplied ECW value relative to measured total body water and total body mass.

Calculation method

ECW as % of total water = measured ECW ÷ measured TBW × 100. ECW as % of body mass = measured ECW ÷ body weight × 100. Use values from the same assessment.

Measurement protocol

Enter the reported ECW, TBW and body weight from one session, using compatible mass units. If the assessment did not report ECW directly, do not infer it from intake or a tape measurement.

Interpretation and limits

This arithmetic does not establish normal fluid balance or diagnose swelling, kidney disease or dehydration. It inherits the source device’s assumptions and variability; compare only like-for-like methods.
