Understand Body Fat Jackson-Pollock 7-Site Calculator +

This estimate uses seven skinfold measurements to predict adult body density and then body-fat percentage. The seven sites are chest, midaxillary, triceps, subscapular, abdomen, suprailiac and thigh.

Calculation method

A sex-specific Jackson–Pollock equation combines the sum of the seven readings with age. Siri conversion is body-fat % = 495 ÷ predicted density − 450. The result is a model estimate, not a direct scan of fat tissue.

Measurement protocol

Use one equation group, measure every site in millimetres with the same caliper and trained technique, and follow the site landmarks of the original protocol. Enter the same age convention and avoid mixing measurements from different assessors or sessions.

Interpretation and limits

The implemented equation bands are for adults: male JP7 ages 18–61 and female JP7 ages 18–55. The original equations were developed in particular adult samples, so accuracy can differ by population, body-fat range, technique and assessor. Do not compare results from different equations as if they were interchangeable.

Jackson AW, Pollock ML. Generalized equations for predicting body density of men. 1978. British Journal of Nutrition; Jackson, Pollock & Ward. Generalized equations for predicting body density of women. 1980. Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise.
