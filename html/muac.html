Mid-Upper Arm Circumference (MUAC) Calculator

ClerFit · Health & Performance Tools

Calculate derived arm measurements

Measure MUAC and triceps skinfold on the same arm and side using a consistent anthropometric protocol. Skinfold thickness is a single-site thickness reading, not a doubled value.

Method and limitations

MUAMC = MUAC − π × triceps skinfold, after converting both lengths to the same unit. Total arm area = MUAC² ÷ (4π); MUAMA = MUAMC² ÷ (4π); estimated arm fat area = total arm area − MUAMA.

The model approximates the arm as concentric circles. Skinfold compressibility, measurement technique and bone size affect accuracy. MUAMA includes the bone area and is not a direct measurement of muscle mass. No age- or sex-based clinical category is assigned.

Understand MUAC +

The MUAC method derives upper-arm muscle and fat-area approximations from mid-upper-arm circumference and a triceps skinfold. It uses a concentric-circle model.

Calculation method

MUAMC = MUAC − π × triceps skinfold. Total arm area = MUAC² ÷ (4π); estimated muscle area = MUAMC² ÷ (4π); estimated fat area = total area − muscle area, with both lengths in the same unit.

Measurement protocol

Measure MUAC at the protocol’s midpoint between shoulder and elbow landmarks and take the triceps skinfold at the matching site. Convert both measurements to the same unit before calculation.

Interpretation and limits

These are geometric anthropometric estimates, not direct tissue measurements. The concentric-circle assumption, skinfold compressibility, bone size and technique affect accuracy. Use age- and population-specific clinical references where required.
