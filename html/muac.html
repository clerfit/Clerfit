Mid-Upper Arm Circumference (MUAC) Calculator

ClerFit · Health & Performance Tools

Calculate derived arm measurements

Measure MUAC and triceps skinfold on the same arm and side using a consistent anthropometric protocol. Skinfold thickness is a single-site thickness reading, not a doubled value.

Method and limitations

MUAMC = MUAC − π × triceps skinfold, after converting both lengths to the same unit. Total arm area = MUAC² ÷ (4π); MUAMA = MUAMC² ÷ (4π); estimated arm fat area = total arm area − MUAMA.

The model approximates the arm as concentric circles. Skinfold compressibility, measurement technique and bone size affect accuracy. MUAMA includes the bone area and is not a direct measurement of muscle mass. No age- or sex-based clinical category is assigned.

How to use it

Measure MUAC at the midpoint between the acromion and olecranon landmarks. Measure triceps skinfold at the corresponding site with an appropriate skinfold caliper, then enter both readings and their units.

Important: Results depend on measurement technique and the quality of the inputs. They are not a substitute for professional advice.
