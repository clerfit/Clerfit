Understand Shoulder-to-Waist Ratio Calculator +

The shoulder-to-waist ratio compares shoulder circumference with waist circumference as a dimensionless proportion.

Calculation method

Ratio = shoulder circumference ÷ waist circumference, after expressing both measurements in the same unit.

Measurement protocol

Measure shoulder circumference with a repeatable tape path and measure waist at a chosen landmark. Keep posture and tape tension consistent; both inputs use one selected unit.

Interpretation and limits

This calculator assigns no universal aesthetic or health target. The result depends on measurement protocol and does not isolate muscle, bone or fat contributions.
