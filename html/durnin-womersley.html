Understand Durnin-Womersley Body Fat Calculator +

The Durnin–Womersley method estimates adult body-fat percentage from four skinfolds: biceps, triceps, subscapular and suprailiac. It uses age- and sex-specific prediction coefficients.

Calculation method

First sum the four readings. The selected coefficient band calculates density as a − b × log10(sum of skinfolds); Siri conversion then gives body-fat % = 495 ÷ density − 450. The original study reported age-group equations for adults.

Measurement protocol

Select the equation group and age band that match the method, then record all four skinfolds in millimetres with a consistent caliper protocol. A sum from mixed units or inconsistent landmarks invalidates the calculation.

Interpretation and limits

The published sample and its age bands define the equation’s evidence; this is not a universal direct measurement. ClerFit accepts ages 16–72 because those are the implemented coefficient bands. Accuracy depends on technique and population fit, and the equation should not be extrapolated to children or unsupported ages.

Durnin JVGA, Womersley J. Body fat assessed from total body density and its estimation from skinfold thickness. 1974. British Journal of Nutrition.
