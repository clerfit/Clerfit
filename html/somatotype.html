Body Type / Somatotype Calculator (Heath–Carter Method)

Somatotype Meaning & Applications

The Heath–Carter somatotype classifies body composition into three components:

Component Meaning Applications
Endomorphy Relative fatness; higher values indicate more body fat accumulation.
  • Focus on fat control and weight management.
  • Cardiovascular fitness programs.
  • Track body composition changes over time.
Mesomorphy Musculoskeletal development; higher values indicate more muscle and stronger bones.
  • Strength and power sports performance.
  • Resistance training programs.
  • Muscle gain or preservation strategies.
Ectomorphy Linearity or slenderness; higher values indicate less fat and muscle mass.
  • Endurance sports potential.
  • Strength-focused training for muscle gain.
  • Nutrition strategies for weight or muscle gain.

Overall Applications

  • Tailor fitness programs based on body type.
  • Guide nutrition strategies for weight, muscle, and fat management.
  • Match body type with sport-specific demands.
  • Monitor body composition changes over time.
Understand Body Shape / Somatotype +

What is Body Shape / Somatotype?

Body Shape / Somatotype is a body measurement or composition indicator. The result depends on the inputs and method used by this calculator.

What does the result mean?

These categories are descriptive models and do not determine health, athletic potential or personality.

Uses and benefits

Can provide a simple vocabulary for discussing body-build patterns.

Limitations and cons

People do not always fit fixed categories, and classifications should not be used to prescribe training or diet on their own.

How to use it

Read each field label and its unit, enter your measurements or values, check that none are missing or implausible, and activate the calculator button. Review the displayed result, formula and limitations. If comparing results over time, use the same measurement method.

Important: ClerFit provides educational calculations. It does not replace professional medical, nutrition or exercise advice. Check the calculator's formula and units before relying on a result.
