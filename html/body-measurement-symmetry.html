Understand Body Measurement Symmetry +

This calculator compares left- and right-side measurements using an absolute difference and a difference relative to their mean.

Calculation method

Absolute difference = |left − right|. Relative difference (%) = |left − right| ÷ ((left + right) ÷ 2) × 100.

Measurement protocol

Measure the same anatomical landmark on both sides, in matching units and posture. Use the same assessor and technique when tracking change.

Interpretation and limits

Human bodies are not perfectly symmetric and small differences can be measurement noise. The number does not diagnose injury, function or a medical condition.
