METs (Metabolic Equivalent of Task)

Definition

METs are a unit that estimates the amount of energy expended during physical activity compared to resting metabolism. 1 MET is the energy cost of sitting quietly, approximately 1 kcal/kg/hour.

Applications

  • Quantifies intensity of physical activities and workouts
  • Used to calculate calorie expenditure during exercise
  • Helps design fitness programs with appropriate intensity
  • Monitors cardiovascular and metabolic health
  • Supports weight management and energy balance calculations
  • Useful in research and clinical exercise prescription
  • Integrated in fitness tracking devices and apps

Related Health Problems

Low MET Activity (Sedentary Lifestyle)

  • Low cardiovascular fitness
  • Increased risk of obesity and metabolic disorders
  • Higher risk of heart disease and type 2 diabetes
  • Muscle weaknes
