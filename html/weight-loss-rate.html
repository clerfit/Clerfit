About Weight Loss Rate

Meaning:

Weight loss rate indicates how much body weight you are expected to lose per week or per month based on your calorie deficit.

Healthy & Safe Rates:

  • 0.25 – 1 kg per week (Safe fat loss)
  • 0.5 – 2 lbs per week

Risks of Too Fast Weight Loss:

  • Muscle loss
  • Hormonal imbalance
  • Nutrient deficiencies
  • Metabolic slowdown

What is Weight Loss Rate?

Weight Loss Rate is a tool for comparing body-weight or circumference changes and planning a goal. The result depends on the inputs and method used by this calculator.

What does the result mean?

Interpret the Weight Loss Rate result in the context of the input values, displayed units and assumptions. Short-term changes can reflect fluid, glycogen and digestive contents, not just fat or muscle. Forecasts assume a rate that may change.

Calculation methodWeight-loss rate = weight change ÷ elapsed time, following the page’s sign convention.
