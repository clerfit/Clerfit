Method and limitations

For circumference C, diameter = C ÷ π, radius = C ÷ (2π), and circle-equivalent area = C² ÷ (4π). If segment length L is entered, the cylindrical volume proxy is area × L.

The optional volume assumes a constant circular cross-section along the entire segment. Real thigh shape and tissue distribution vary; the output is not muscle volume, body composition, or a clinical assessment.

Understand Mid-Thigh Circumference +

This tool converts a measured mid-thigh circumference to circle-equivalent diameter, radius and cross-sectional area. An optional segment length gives a simple cylinder volume proxy.

Calculation method

Area = circumference² ÷ (4π). If segment length is entered, volume proxy = area × length. Units follow the circumference and segment-length selections.

Measurement protocol

Measure at a clearly defined mid-thigh landmark and use an optional length from the same segment. Keep circumference and segment length units compatible before interpreting volume.

Interpretation and limits

The thigh is tapered and non-circular; a cylinder assumes constant cross-section. The outputs are geometry proxies, not muscle volume, fat volume or a clinical measurement.
