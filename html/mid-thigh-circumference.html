Method and limitations

For circumference C, diameter = C ÷ π, radius = C ÷ (2π), and circle-equivalent area = C² ÷ (4π). If segment length L is entered, the cylindrical volume proxy is area × L.

The optional volume assumes a constant circular cross-section along the entire segment. Real thigh shape and tissue distribution vary; the output is not muscle volume, body composition, or a clinical assessment.

How to use it

Measure the thigh at a consistently defined midpoint and enter its circumference. Segment length is optional and is used only for the simplified cylinder-volume calculation.

Important: Results depend on measurement technique and the quality of the inputs. They are not a substitute for professional advice.
