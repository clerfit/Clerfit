Metabolic Age / Resting Energy Estimate

What is Metabolic Age?

Metabolic age compares your Resting Metabolic Rate (RMR) to the average RMR of your age group and gender. If your metabolic age is lower than your actual age, your metabolism is more efficient than average; if higher, it may indicate slower metabolism.

Applications of Metabolic Age

  • Track metabolic health and fitness level.
  • Assess effects of weight loss or strength training.
  • Guide nutrition and calorie planning.
  • Monitor aging and lifestyle impact on metabolism.
  • Compare metabolic efficiency across peers.
Understand Metabolic Age +

What is Metabolic Age?

There is no universally validated formula that converts an estimated resting metabolic rate into an individual metabolic age. This page reports a resting-energy estimate instead of inventing a biological-age score.

What does the result mean?

The resting-energy estimate is a population equation and can differ from measured energy expenditure. It is not a measure of biological age or metabolic health.

Uses and benefits

It can provide a rough resting-energy estimate when interpreted with its limitations.

Limitations and cons

A validated metabolic-age score is not calculated; actual resting energy expenditure varies and should not be inferred from a sparse average-age table.
