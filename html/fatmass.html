Fat Mass

Fat Mass is the total weight of all the fat tissue in the body, including both essential fat needed for normal body functions and storage fat. It is an important indicator of health and body composition.

Fat Mass – Applications

  • Calculates estimated fat mass from body weight and an entered body-fat percentage
  • Used in weight management programs
  • Helps design diet and exercise plans
  • Tracks progress in fat loss or gain programs
  • Used by nutritionists and fitness trainers
  • Provides better insight than BMI alone
  • Essential for body composition analysis

Related Health Problems

Low Fat Mass

  • Hormonal imbalance
  • Weak immunity
  • Fatigue and low energy
  • Risk of nutrient deficiencies
  • Fertility issues in severe cases

High Fat Mass

  • Obesity and overweight
  • Heart disease risk
  • Type 2 diabetes
  • High blood pressure
  • Joint pain and mobility problems
