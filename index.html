Frequently asked questions

Open a question only when you need the answer. All answers start collapsed to keep the page tidy.

Are ClerFit calculators free to use?

Yes. The calculators available on this website are provided for people to explore without a subscription.

How accurate are the results?

Results depend on the inputs, formula and measurement method. Many health, fitness and performance values are estimates, not laboratory measurements. Review the explanation and limitations shown with each calculator.

Can a calculator diagnose a medical condition?

No. ClerFit is an educational tool and does not provide a medical diagnosis or replace a qualified health professional.

How do I find a specific calculator?

Open the three-line menu to search calculator names, keywords and categories, or use Explore by Topic on this page to browse a category.

Can I switch between metric and imperial units?

Use the unit preference in the calculator sidebar where supported. Individual calculators may also provide their own unit selectors. Always check the unit displayed beside an input or result.

What should I do if a result looks wrong?

Check the values and units you entered, review the formula and assumptions, and try again. If the issue remains, use Contact Us to report the calculator name, inputs and what you expected.

Does ClerFit store my measurements?

Do not enter information you do not want processed by your browser or any service a page explicitly uses. Review the page's notices before submitting information. This static calculator website does not promise a personal measurement-history service unless a page explicitly says otherwise.

Use estimates thoughtfully

ClerFit provides educational estimates, not medical diagnoses. Interpretation may depend on age, measurement method and personal context. Consult a qualified professional for health concerns.
