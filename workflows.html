Body composition and nutrition

Move from a starting measurement to an estimate of daily energy needs and a nutrition-planning tool.

  1. BMI Calculator Optional starting measure; BMI is not a body-fat measurement.
  2. BMR Calculator Estimate resting energy expenditure using the method shown on the page.
  3. TDEE Calculator Explore daily energy expenditure using an activity factor.
  4. Calorie Deficit Calculator Review energy-balance scenarios cautiously; avoid aggressive targets.
  5. Macro Split Calculator Explore a macronutrient distribution and its assumptions.

Body-fat estimates can be used as a separate starting point: Body Fat Percentage.

Strength and lifting

Estimate a repetition maximum, examine training volume, then compare relative strength.

  1. One-Rep Max Calculator Estimate a maximum from a supported load and repetition range.
  2. Training Volume Calculator Explore sets, repetitions and load; this is not a programme prescription.
  3. Relative Strength Calculator Enter body weight and the relevant lift to compare relative strength.

Running and cardio

Use a field-test estimate as context, review heart-rate information, then calculate pace from measured performance.

  1. Cooper Test Calculator Enter the distance covered in a 12-minute test.
  2. VO₂ Max Estimate Review the equation estimate and its limitations.
  3. Heart Rate Calculator Explore age- and resting-heart-rate-based estimates where supported.
  4. Running Pace Calculator Calculate pace from a measured distance and elapsed time.

Field-test estimates vary with pacing, terrain, weather, health and measurement quality. Stop exercise if you experience concerning symptoms and seek appropriate professional advice.
