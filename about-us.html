About ClerFit

Practical tools. Clear methods. Better-informed choices.

Why ClerFit exists

ClerFit is an independently developed resource for health, fitness, body-measurement, nutrition and performance calculations. Its purpose is to make useful calculations easier to access and understand, with clear inputs, readable results and explanations of the methods behind them.

ClerFit is an educational digital resource. It is not a hospital, medical practice, diagnostic service, university programme or professional licensing body.

How we approach calculations

Where a recognised equation, guideline or research method is used, we aim to identify its source and explain relevant assumptions. Calculations should use clearly labelled units, sensible input boundaries and reproducible arithmetic. Results are estimates whenever the underlying method estimates rather than directly measures a value.

Some tools provide descriptive ratios or site-defined arithmetic rather than a clinically validated score. They should not be presented as diagnoses, medical risk classifications, attractiveness ratings or universal performance rankings.

Who creates and reviews ClerFit?

ClerFit is independently built and maintained. We do not claim that the website or its calculators have been certified, endorsed or clinically approved by a medical institution or professional organisation.

A source citation, automated test or internal review is not the same as review by a qualified clinician, registered dietitian, accredited exercise professional or other subject-matter expert. We will identify a professional reviewer only when that person has actually reviewed the relevant material and their credentials, review date and scope can be stated accurately. No professional-review badge should be inferred otherwise.

Privacy by design

ClerFit does not require an account, login or registration. It does not offer saved health profiles or a personal measurement-history feature. Calculator inputs are intended to be processed in your browser and are not intentionally sent to a ClerFit measurement database. Hosting providers may still process routine technical request data, and the optional contact form uses a separate delivery provider. Read the Privacy Notice for details and limitations.

Corrections and feedback

If you find a calculation, explanation, accessibility issue or broken reference that needs attention, report the page and describe the issue through the Contact page. Please use example values rather than sending medical records or sensitive personal information.

Important information

ClerFit provides general educational information, not personal medical advice. A calculator cannot account for every health condition, medication, measurement method or individual circumstance. For decisions affecting health, consult an appropriately qualified professional. See the Educational Disclaimer and Terms of Use.
