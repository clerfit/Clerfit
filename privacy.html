Privacy Notice

Last updated: 16 October 2026

1. Calculator inputs

ClerFit is designed so calculator inputs and results are processed in your browser by the page's JavaScript. The site code does not intentionally send calculator inputs or results to a ClerFit database, and this release does not include analytics scripts or advertising trackers. This is a description of the code in this release, not a guarantee about the separate hosting platform, your browser, extensions, network provider, or any future changes.

2. Contact form

If you voluntarily submit the contact form, the name, email address and message you provide are transmitted to Formspree, our contact-form service, so the message can be delivered. Formspree and the hosting provider may process technical information under their own terms and privacy practices. Do not include medical records, passwords, financial details, or other sensitive information in a message. ClerFit cannot independently promise the provider's retention period or deletion practices; review the provider's current policy before submitting.

3. Storage, cookies and analytics

The calculator code in this release is not intended to save calculator entries in local storage or session storage. Embedded Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager scripts have been removed from calculator pages. Static hosting may still process server logs, IP addresses, request metadata, and security-related information. Browser features, extensions, and network services outside ClerFit's control may also process data.

4. Third-party services

The contact form uses Formspree only when you submit it. Clicking external reference links takes you to the destination website, which has its own privacy practices. We do not claim that third-party services collect no information.

5. Data minimisation and your choices

Do not enter identifying details into calculators; they are generally unnecessary. Contact us only if you choose to, and provide the minimum information needed. You may use calculators without submitting the contact form. If you contact us about a message, include enough detail to identify it without sending additional sensitive data.

6. Children's privacy

ClerFit is not designed to collect children's personal information. Health and body-related outputs are educational estimates only. A parent or qualified professional should help interpret health-related information for minors.

7. Changes and contact

This notice may be updated if the website or its providers change. Re-check it before relying on the site. See the Terms of Use and Educational Disclaimer.
