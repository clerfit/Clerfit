CLERFIT · PRIVACY

Privacy Notice

Last updated: 26 November 2026

1. No accounts or cloud profiles

ClerFit does not offer account creation, login, registration, cloud-synchronised profiles or a server-side personal health-measurement history. You can use calculators without creating an account. ClerFit does not ask you to provide a name, email address, date of birth or other identity details to calculate a result.

2. Calculator inputs and results

The calculator code processes values you enter in your browser. Calculator inputs and results are not sent to a ClerFit measurement database. If you explicitly enable the optional “Save compatible inputs on this device” control on a calculator page, compatible age, height and weight inputs may be saved in that browser’s local storage so they can prefill matching fields on later visits. This is optional and off by default. Do not enter identifying information into calculator fields; use only the measurements required by the calculation.

This describes ClerFit's website code, not every component of the wider internet. Your browser, extensions, device, network provider and hosting platform operate separately. The static hosting provider may process request logs, IP addresses, device/browser details and security information under its own policies.

3. Browser storage, cookies and analytics

Persistent local storage is used only after you actively opt in on a calculator page. The saved fields are limited to compatible age, sex/reference selection, height and weight inputs; results, names, email addresses and full calculation histories are not saved. Data stays in that browser on that device, is not synchronised and can be cleared with the “Clear saved inputs” control or by clearing site data in your browser. The saved profile expires after 90 days. Disabling the checkbox clears the saved profile.

When you follow a calculator’s next-step link, compatible inputs are temporarily transferred through session storage for that tab so they do not appear in the URL, browser history or referrer. This temporary handoff expires after ten minutes and is removed when the destination page reads it. Unit preferences remain specific to each calculator; ClerFit does not apply a global unit setting. The site code does not intentionally use analytics or advertising trackers. Browser settings, extensions and platform-level services may behave independently of ClerFit.

4. Contact form and third-party processing

If you choose to submit the Contact Us form, the name, email address and message you enter are sent to Formspree, our contact-form delivery provider, so the message can be delivered. This is separate from calculator processing. Do not include medical records, diagnoses, medication details, passwords, financial information or other sensitive personal information in a contact message. Formspree and the hosting provider may process technical information under their own terms and privacy practices. ClerFit cannot independently guarantee their retention periods or deletion procedures; review their current policies before submitting.

5. External links

Scientific references and other external links take you to websites operated by other organisations. Those organisations control their own sites, cookies, logs and privacy practices. A reference link does not mean that the organisation endorses ClerFit.

6. Data minimisation and your choices

You may use calculators without enabling on-device input reuse or submitting the contact form. Persistent browser saving is off by default and can be disabled or cleared at any time using the controls on a calculator page. Provide only the information necessary for a calculation or message. If you do not want a third-party contact provider to receive your name, email and message, do not submit the contact form. You can still use the calculators without an account.

7. Children's privacy and health information

ClerFit does not provide child accounts or collect profile information for children. Some health and body-related methods have age restrictions or require age- and sex-specific reference standards. Outputs are educational estimates, not medical advice. A parent or qualified professional should help interpret health-related information for minors.

8. Changes and contact

This notice may be updated if the website code, hosting arrangement or service providers change. Check it periodically. For privacy questions, use the Contact page without including sensitive health information. Also read the Terms of Use and Educational Disclaimer.

This notice describes the current intended website behaviour and is not a guarantee about independent third-party services or a substitute for legal advice. ClerFit should review this notice whenever its hosting, contact service or code changes.
