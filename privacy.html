CLERFIT · PRIVACY

Privacy Notice

Last updated: 15 November 2026

1. No accounts or saved health profiles

ClerFit does not offer account creation, login, registration, saved user profiles or a personal health-measurement history. You can use calculators without creating an account. ClerFit does not ask you to provide a name, email address, date of birth or other identity details to calculate a result.

2. Calculator inputs and results

The calculator code is designed to process values you enter in your browser. The current site code does not intentionally transmit calculator inputs or results to a ClerFit measurement database or save them as a personal profile. Do not enter identifying information into calculator fields; use only the measurements required by the calculation.

This describes ClerFit's website code, not every component of the wider internet. Your browser, extensions, device, network provider and hosting platform operate separately. The static hosting provider may process request logs, IP addresses, device/browser details and security information under its own policies.

3. Browser storage, cookies and analytics

ClerFit does not provide a feature that saves health measurements in browser storage or synchronises them between devices. The calculator experience is not intended to store entries in local storage or session storage. The site code for this release does not intentionally use analytics or advertising trackers. Browser settings, extensions and platform-level services may behave independently of ClerFit.

4. Contact form and third-party processing

If you choose to submit the Contact Us form, the name, email address and message you enter are sent to Formspree, our contact-form delivery provider, so the message can be delivered. This is separate from calculator processing. Do not include medical records, diagnoses, medication details, passwords, financial information or other sensitive personal information in a contact message. Formspree and the hosting provider may process technical information under their own terms and privacy practices. ClerFit cannot independently guarantee their retention periods or deletion procedures; review their current policies before submitting.

5. External links

Scientific references and other external links take you to websites operated by other organisations. Those organisations control their own sites, cookies, logs and privacy practices. A reference link does not mean that the organisation endorses ClerFit.

6. Data minimisation and your choices

You may use calculators without submitting the contact form. Provide only the information necessary for a calculation or message. If you do not want a third-party contact provider to receive your name, email and message, do not submit the contact form. You can still use the calculators without an account.

7. Children's privacy and health information

ClerFit does not provide child accounts or collect profile information for children. Some health and body-related methods have age restrictions or require age- and sex-specific reference standards. Outputs are educational estimates, not medical advice. A parent or qualified professional should help interpret health-related information for minors.

8. Changes and contact

This notice may be updated if the website code, hosting arrangement or service providers change. Check it periodically. For privacy questions, use the Contact page without including sensitive health information. Also read the Terms of Use and Educational Disclaimer.

This notice describes the current intended website behaviour and is not a guarantee about independent third-party services or a substitute for legal advice. ClerFit should review this notice whenever its hosting, contact service or code changes.
