Educational Disclaimer

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Educational tools, not medical advice

ClerFit calculators and content are provided for general educational and informational purposes only. They are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, mitigate or prevent any disease or medical condition, and they do not replace advice from a doctor, dietitian, physiotherapist, pharmacist or other qualified professional.

Estimates and assumptions

Outputs may be estimates based on published equations, selected activity factors, user-entered measurements or site-defined descriptive methods. Published equations have limits and may not apply to all ages, populations, health conditions or circumstances. Face, posture, performance, fatigue and recovery ratios are descriptive indicators unless explicitly supported by an established reference; they are not diagnoses or validated universal rankings.

Precaution and safety

Do not use a calculator result as a guarantee of safety, a safe exposure duration, an injury prediction, a medical report or an individualized treatment plan. Heat, cold, UV exposure, hydration, nutrition, exercise and health risks depend on factors not captured by a simple calculator. For urgent symptoms or dangerous environmental conditions, follow local emergency guidance and qualified advice.

Minors and health-related outputs

Children and teenagers require age-appropriate interpretation for many body measurements and health estimates. Adult reference categories must not be applied to minors when age- and sex-specific standards are required. Seek a qualified professional when a result may affect a health decision.

Report a possible error

If a formula or result appears incorrect, do not rely on it. Send the calculator name, input units, and a non-sensitive example through the contact page. Do not send medical records or sensitive personal details.
